L101: # ACTION:
L102: 
L103: Notice of revised fiscal year (FY) 2025 Fair Market Rents (FMRs).
L104: 
L105: # SUMMARY:
L106: 
L107: This notice updates the FY 2025 FMRs for several areas based on new survey data.
L108: 
L109: # DATES:
L110: 
L111: The revised FY 2025 FMRs are effective on April 28, 2025.
L112: # FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
L113: Adam Bibler, telephone 202-402-6057. Questions related to the use of FMRs or voucher payment standards should be directed to the respective local HUD program staff.
L114: For technical information on the methodology used to develop FMRs or a listing of all FMRs, please call the HUD USER information line at 800-245-2691 (toll-free), email the Program Parameters and Research Division at pprd@hud.gov, or access the information on the HUD USER website: cite37†http://www.huduser.gov/​portal/​datasets/​fmr.html†www.huduser.gov .
L115: HUD welcomes and is prepared to receive calls from individuals who are deaf or hard of hearing, as well as individuals with speech or communication disabilities. To learn more about how to make an accessible telephone call, please visit cite38†https://www.fcc.gov/​consumers/​guides/​telecommunications-relay-service-trs†www.fcc.gov .
L116: # SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:
L117: 
L118: On August 14, 2024, HUD published in the Federal Register the FY 2025 FMRs, requested comments on the FY 2025 FMRs, and outlined procedures for requesting a reevaluation of an area's FY 2025 FMRs, available at cite39†89 FR 66127 . This notice revises FY 2025 FMRs for several areas based on data provided to HUD.

L189: ## II. Environmental Impact
L190: 
L191: This notice involves the establishment of Fair Market Rent schedules and does not constitute a development decision affecting the physical condition of specific project areas or building sites. Accordingly, under cite42†24 CFR 50.19(c)(6)†www.ecfr.gov , this notice is categorically excluded from environmental review under the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (cite43†42 U.S.C. 4321†www.govinfo.gov ).
