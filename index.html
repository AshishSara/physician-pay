New to physician pay? Salary, billings and take-home mean different things.

Gross billings / revenue

Money a practice receives before paying for staff, rent and other practice costs. Canadian clinical payment benchmarks start here.

Salary / compensation

Pay to a physician before personal tax. The employee model assumes the employer covers practice overhead. Check which benefits a compensation figure includes.

Personal take-home

Money left after practice costs, taxes and the insurance or professional costs included here. Housing, food and debt payments come next.

Purchasing power

A modeled estimate of what take-home can buy at local prices. Its I$ units measure spending power; they are not dollars deposited in your bank account.

A median is the middle reported value. It does not mean a new graduate, a full-time attending or a physician with your workload will earn that amount.

Understand incorporationCompare personal income separately from money held in a corporation

It depends on the work arrangement

Incorporation eligibility and tax treatment depend on local professional rules and how you work. An employee salary does not become eligible business income simply because a physician creates a corporation. Canadian personal-services-business rules can change the tax treatment of employment-like arrangements.

CRA: personal services businesses · CPSO: professional corporations

Corporate funds and personal cash are separate

Money retained in a corporation is not personal take-home. For a Canadian corporation, retaining eligible business profits can defer personal tax until funds are paid out. That timing benefit does not guarantee permanent tax savings. U.S. rules differ; S-corporation income generally passes through to shareholders for personal taxation.

CRA: corporate and dividend taxation · IRS: S corporations

How to use an incorporated scenario here

Choose Enter my own income → Known personal take-home. Enter personal cash after all applicable taxes, practice costs and insurance, before household spending. Exclude funds retained in the corporation. Use the same withdrawal and household-spending assumptions when comparing places.

Illustration: if you have $150,000 available personally after tax and costs, and a further $50,000 stays in the corporation, enter $150,000 in the selected currency. The retained $50,000 belongs in a separate corporate wealth analysis.

This route compares the personal take-home you supply. It does not evaluate the total financial benefit of incorporation. Fixed tax overrides do not add corporate accounting to the automatic model.

How everything is calculated

All calculations run in your browser. Inputs stay on your device unless you export them. Physician Pay supports comparisons using published evidence and editable assumptions. It does not predict an individual salary or provide a personalized tax return. Pay, taxes and living costs are only part of choosing a specialty or place to practise.
